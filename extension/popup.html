✦ CourseLark

Your campus, at your pace.

Data in this browser: When you visit your school site, CourseLark automatically stores your Canvas profile identifier, courses, assignments, grades, recent announcements, and any notes, files, and study activity you add in this browser. It does not send these records to Noah Fishman.

Optional transfers: AI, browser speech, calendar links and calendar sync, and custom images may contact outside services when you choose those features.

Before CourseLark reads your school sites, read the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.

By selecting Agree and continue, you agree to the Terms. This does not replace any permission required for a child to use optional services.

CourseLark recognizes Canvas, Brightspace, Moodle, Blackboard and Google Classroom pages by themselves and reads your courses, assignments and grades through your signed-in session each time you visit, at most every 30 minutes. Other pages are checked for LMS markers and left alone. Data stays in this browser; it never submits work.
